## 1. Executive Summary

The NOTC Technology Platform is the central digital system supporting the planning, mobilisation, registration, monitoring, reporting, administration, and leadership oversight of A Night of a Thousand Crusades.

The platform gives ministers, zones, groups, churches, cells, networks, volunteers, media personnel, and administrators dedicated tools for their respective responsibilities. It also gives leadership a consolidated view of crusades planned, participating stakeholders, reports received, expected attendance, geographical coverage, outcomes, pending work, and special NOTC initiatives.

The solution currently provides:

- A public NOTC campaign website and information centre.
- Crusade registration for zones, groups, churches, cells, and networks.
- Post-crusade reporting with results, impact figures, photographs, and media links.
- Live registration monitoring and analysis dashboards.
- Private dashboards for individual zones and networks.
- Coverage analysis showing registered and unregistered zones and groups.
- Detailed analysis by crusade type, zone, group, church, and cell.
- Special-purpose modules for mission nations, upcoming international crusades, missions trips, media training, approved resources, and Loveworld Blue Elite.
- Administrative editing, confirmation, assignment, filtering, deletion, and export facilities.
- KingsChat-based access control and page-level administrative permissions.
- Database backup, recovery, and operational protection facilities.

## 3. Public Website and Campaign Experience

The public website is the main digital entry point for NOTC. It presents the campaign vision, countdown, programme information, participation opportunities, approved materials, registration links, and sponsorship access.

### Home page

The home page provides:

- NOTC campaign identity and central message.
- Countdown to the main NOTC event.
- Road-to-NOTC programme calendar.
- Direct access to crusade registration.
- Links to all active NOTC initiatives.
- Preview of approved campaign resources.
- A dedicated sponsorship section explaining how partnership supports crusades, outreach materials, equipment, Bibles, and spiritual materials.

### Accessibility and international reach

The public platform is responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Public pages include translation support so users in different nations can interact with the platform in an appropriate language. Important ministry terms are protected with approved translations where required.

## 4. Crusade Registration

The registration system enables ministry stakeholders to register each planned crusade as an individual event rather than submitting only a broad total.

### Stakeholders supported

- Zones
- Groups
- Churches
- Cells
- Ministry networks

### Information captured

For every registration, the platform can capture:

- Responsible ministry unit and full ministry hierarchy.
- Nation and city.
- Crusade type and format.
- Crusade name.
- Event date and venue.
- Expected attendance.
- Minister information.
- Collaborating zones, networks, or ministry departments.
- Budget and zone contribution information.
- Rhapsody copies and mobilisation information.
- Permit status.
- Media coverage plans.
- Contact and submission details.

Multiple crusades can be entered in one submission, while each remains separately identifiable, editable, reportable, and measurable.

### Live ministry directory

Zone and group choices are supplied in real time from the official Churches API. Additions, edits, and deletions made in the ministry directory are reflected in the portal without maintaining a separate static list. The directory is used for current ministry selection and coverage monitoring, while previously submitted records remain preserved as historical data.

## 6. Administration and Leadership Dashboards

The administration portal is a protected operational workspace. It contains the following dashboards and management areas.

### 6.1 Live Registrations Dashboard

This dashboard provides the current global registration position, including:

- Total registrations.
- Total individual crusades planned.
- Participating zones, groups, churches, cells, and networks.
- Countries, continents, and cities represented.
- Expected attendance.
- Confirmed crusades.
- Reports received.
- Crusades awaiting reports.
- Crusades by type.
- Crusades by ministry level.
- Crusades by country and continent.
- Crusades by zone, group, church, cell, and network.
- Recent registration activity.

Dashboard figures are interactive. Selecting a figure opens the underlying filtered records for review.

### 6.2 Crusade Analysis

The Crusade Analysis page provides a more detailed operational breakdown.

It includes:

- Crusade types by zone.
- Mega, cellular, online, and other active crusade categories.
- Total crusades registered by each zone.
- Cellular crusades by zone, group, and church.
- A dedicated Cell Breakdown organised by **Zone → Group → Church → Cell**.
- Number of distinct cells that registered crusades in each zone.
- Number of cells under each group.
- Registered crusades and registration entries for every cell.
- Search and direct drill-down into the corresponding registration records.

### 6.3 Registration Records

The detailed registration table enables authorised administrators to:

- Search registrations.
- Apply multiple filters.
- Review every individual crusade.
- Review contact and ministry information.
- Edit future crusade plans.
- Confirm readiness or indicate that a crusade is not holding.
- Provide confirmation feedback.
- Delete unwanted or incorrect records where authorised.
- Open the corresponding stakeholder dashboard.
- Export filtered records.

Planning changes are controlled by date so past crusade plans are not casually altered after the event.

### 6.4 Reports Dashboard

The reports dashboard presents actual crusade outcomes and supports:

- Global outcome totals.
- Attendance and online reach.
- Salvation and follow-up metrics.
- Results by crusade type.
- Results by zone, group, church, cell, network, country, and city.
- Planned-versus-held comparisons.
- Geographical display of reported activity.
- Recent reports.
- Customisable dashboard layout.
- Drill-down from summary widgets to supporting records.

### 6.5 Detailed Crusade Reports

The detailed reports area allows administrators to:

- Search and filter completed crusades.
- Review all impact metrics.
- View submitted media evidence.
- Edit reports where permission has been granted.
- Remove incorrect reports where authorised.
- Export the selected data.

### 6.6 Zone and Group Coverage

The Coverage page compares live registration data with the official Churches API directory.

It shows:

- Total zones and groups in the current ministry directory.
- Zones and groups that have registered crusades.
- Zones and groups that have not registered crusades.
- Percentage participation.
- Crusades registered by each ministry unit.
- Region information.
- Search and registered/not-registered filters.

Because this view uses the live Churches API as its authority, deleted directory entries are removed from the current coverage list and newly added entries appear automatically.

### 6.7 Country Coverage

The Country Coverage page provides leadership-level geographical analysis, including:

- Total countries represented.
- Countries with registrations.
- Countries without registrations.
- Coverage by GPD zones and ministry networks.
- Crusades and registrations by country.
- Continental grouping.
- Lists for mobilisation follow-up.

### 6.8 Zone and Network Dashboard Links

Administrators can generate secure private dashboard links for every current zone and network.

Each private dashboard allows the relevant stakeholder to see only its own:

- Registrations.
- Crusade plans.
- Readiness status.
- Reports.
- Results and totals.
- Pending reporting responsibilities.

The links can be copied individually or exported in bulk. Regenerating a link invalidates the former link. Deleted zones are no longer shown in the current admin link directory.

### 6.9 Manual Ministry Directory Reconciliation

When a group was entered manually because it was not yet available in the official directory, authorised administrators can later map it to the correct live directory entry. This maintains accurate ministry-level reporting without discarding the original submission.

## 7. Special NOTC Initiative Modules

### 7.1 National Missions Leadership Initiative

Ministers can indicate a preferred mission nation outside the nation where their zone or ministry is located.

The administrative module supports:

- Review of all submitted preferences.
- Search, filters, and sorting.
- Assignment or reassignment of final nations.
- Monitoring preferences versus final assignments.
- Opening or closing the selection window.
- Removal of incorrect submissions.
- Export for leadership review.

### 7.2 Upcoming Crusades

The Upcoming Crusades module publishes planned international crusades as individual selectable opportunities, organised by continent.

It provides:

- Nation, city, crusade name, crusade date, and suggested arrival date.
- Selection of multiple individual crusades.
- Passport-country information.
- Zone and optional group details.
- Sponsorship and travel-expense information.
- Search by nation, city, or crusade.
- Clear indication when a crusade has already been assigned.

Administrators can:

- Review expressions of interest.
- Search and filter submissions.
- Mark a crusade as assigned so it is unavailable for further selection.
- Edit crusade information.
- Remove opportunities that should no longer appear.
- Export submitted interests.

### 7.3 Global Missions Trip Volunteer Mobilisation

The missions-trip module captures volunteers who are available to serve in other nations.

It records:

- Personal and designation details.
- Zone, group, and church information.
- Passport country and additional passports.
- Preferred destination.
- Independent travel access and availability.
- Sponsorship interest.
- Additional information.

Administrators can review, search, filter, sort, remove, and export applications.

### 7.4 Global Media Training Mobilisation

This module registers media personnel and volunteers for NOTC media training.

It captures:

- Zone, group, and church.
- City and nation.
- Participant details.
- Media role.
- Languages spoken.
- Contact information.

Administrators can filter by zone and role, review individual participants, remove incorrect registrations, and export results.

### 7.5 Approved Resources Hub

The public Resources Hub provides approved materials for mobilisation, teaching, outreach, media, and crusade execution.

Authorised administrators can:

- Create resource categories.
- Upload approved files.
- Publish external links.
- Add or automatically discover preview images.
- Edit titles, descriptions, categories, and visibility.
- Reorder resources.
- Remove outdated materials.

Uploaded resources can be as large as 150 MB.

### 7.6 Campaign Avatar Creator

The avatar tool allows participants to:

- Upload a personal photograph.
- Position and scale it within the approved NOTC frame.
- Keep the image constrained within the frame.
- Download a finished campaign avatar for sharing.

### 7.7 Loveworld Blue Elite Module

The Blue Elite module is a separate registration and administrative area for Loveworld Blue Elite staff.

It provides:

- Dedicated Blue Elite registration.
- Department-level tracking.
- Separate dashboard statistics.
- Detailed registration records.
- Search, filters, editing, deletion, and export.
- Data separation from the main public NOTC registration totals.

## 10. Data Protection and Continuity

The platform includes operational safeguards designed to protect campaign records.

These include:

- A central transactional database for registrations, reports, and administrative data.
- Automatic rolling database backups during important data changes.
- Verified database snapshots.
- Backup integrity checks.
- Retention of recent and longer-term recovery points.
- A protected administration page for database backup and recovery operations.
- A staged restore process that checks a database before replacement.
- Preservation of a pre-restore recovery copy.
- Secure storage of administrative session tokens as hashes rather than readable browser tokens.
- One-week administrative sessions with immediate invalidation on logout.
- Restricted access to uploaded report photographs.

The system preserves historical registrations and reports even when the live Churches API directory changes. Current directory and coverage screens use the latest official structure, while completed submissions remain available as an audit trail.
